HOW TO PREPARE

Make the most of your consultation.

To help us hit the ground running, here are a few items that are helpful to have in mind before our meeting starts.

Current Accounting Tools

Know which software you use (QBO, Xero, NetSuite, Excel) and whether you have current administrative access.

Rough Monthly Volume

An approximate count of bank accounts, credit cards, monthly transactions, and regular billing invoices.

Upcoming Deadlines

Any pending tax filings, board meetings, funding rounds, or fiscal year-end closes on your near horizon.

Primary Pain Points

Where your internal team spends the most manual hours, or what reporting metrics you currently lack.
