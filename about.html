Our Origin

Built on trust and relentless accuracy

Stackly started with a straightforward observation: most growing enterprises receive accounting that looks backwards, rather than strategic guidance that propels them forward. Traditional firms file annual tax forms, but few act as true copilot partners.

We built a new model of financial advisory—one that unites senior CPA acumen, proactive quarterly tax structuring, and real-time cloud technology into a single, cohesive partnership.

Today, we advise hundreds of high-growth companies across the country, helping them safeguard operating margins, eliminate compliance anxiety, and navigate capital growth with complete clarity.

Our Mission
